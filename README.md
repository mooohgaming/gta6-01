Solmar Bay is an open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built from scratch in plain JavaScript and canvas — no libraries, no assets, no network. A seeded 2560x2080 city with six named landmarks, traffic and pedestrians, stealable cars, a 0–5 star wanted system with foot cops and police Interceptors, pistol combat, pickups, a HUD with a live minimap, a save, and a six-mission story arc: a cafe job, a timed pizza run, stealing the red Comet GT, losing a three-star tail, a turf war with the Vultures, and the Golden Palm heist. All original: the city, the characters, the vehicles, the story and the sound are ours.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. WASD or arrows drive, Space is the handbrake (and starts the game), E enters and exits cars, F or click shoots, R reloads, M mutes, P pauses. Your progress saves in the browser, so you can continue the story on your next visit.
